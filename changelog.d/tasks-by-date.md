### TASKS lists its work by day

##### Changed

- TASKS, an agent's and a channel's, lists its plans and tasks under
  the day they last moved on, newest first, headed as ARCHIVE heads its
  days: TODAY, YESTERDAY, then the date. Within a day the plan being
  worked on comes first, then the other plans by when they last moved
  on, then the day's work of no plan under its own No Plan header.
- A plan is listed once, with all its todos and tasks, under the day
  its file or one of its tasks last changed, however many days its work
  took. An agent's todo list carried over from one request to the next
  is kept together the same way. Work of no known time, such as a cloud
  agent's plan that nothing has worked on yet, goes last, under
  EARLIER.
- The highlight passes over the day headers, which take no clicks.
  Each day's No Plan folds on its own.
