### A reply's paragraphs are set apart

##### Fixed

- In a chat, a blank row now goes between the paragraphs of an agent's
  reply. Agents often start a paragraph on the next line with no empty
  line before it, and those paragraphs ran together. This holds in every
  agent's chat and in a channel's chat, for the coordinator's replies
  and the members' posts. Your own messages keep their line breaks as
  typed.
