### Members' final reports reach the coordinator, once and on time

##### Fixed

- A member's report on an assignment often never reached whoever
  assigned it, and some came one message late, answering an earlier
  request. The report was sent only if the assignment's message was
  still in the member's conversation when its work ended. A long
  conversation summarizes its older messages to save room, which
  removed it. For a cloud member, a failed copy of the conversation
  left an earlier turn's, so the report waited and the next turn
  answered it with its own words. Now the turn that starts on an
  assignment answers it, with what that turn (or the software
  factory's last turn after it) said last; a cloud turn uses the reply
  it streamed, even when its conversation could not be copied.
